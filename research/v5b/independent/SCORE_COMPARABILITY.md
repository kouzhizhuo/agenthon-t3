# Independent score-comparability assessment

The numbers 13,797.7076 and 212,671.4577 differ arithmetically by about 15.41x, but the local repository does **not** establish a 15.41x competitiveness gap. Such a claim requires evidence that both numbers use the same ranked scorer, host hardware, scenario roster and seeds, warmups/repeats, aggregation and process timing scope. We did not inspect those two board submissions' trusted per-unit timing records here.

Current Track 3 production scoring ranks admissible submissions by raw events/sec descending. `telemetry.ranked_timing` takes the Runner's parquet-footer event count, requires rankable measured repeats and exact output-tree/event-count agreement with the scored run, then computes the median of `n_events / repeat.elapsed_sec`. The elapsed duration is host-measured. Production has no participant-sidecar fallback. The README specifies a median over the evaluation roster; bootstrap grouping is by scenario family. Batch numerator is the sum of declared isolated sub-scenario trace counts, with one aggregate host duration. This is not a per-market score or sum of each sub's EPS.

The separate developer verifier is explicitly non-rankable and may use local host metrics or participant `events_per_sec` as a fallback. Its numbers cannot be assumed comparable with production. A participant's self-reported rate measures its chosen interval and is only cross-checked for internal consistency and row count. The reference sidecars' timers cover the simulation loop rather than full process startup/import/write/shutdown; the hardware behind those historical sidecars was not recorded.

`domain.py` explicitly states that historical figures of about 65,000 baseline EPS and 400,000 vectorized-reference EPS were not measured on the evaluation fleet, and that the 150,000–600,000 competitive band was withdrawn as fabricated. It reports shipped baseline sidecars around 13,793 geometric mean EPS on unrecorded hardware and loop-only timing. The numerical proximity of 13,797.7076 to that historical aggregate does not prove it is the same measurement or formula. Geometric mean reference diagnostics also differ from the board's documented median aggregation.

The local task notes describe a 4-vCPU / 16GiB / B200 gVisor box, whereas our native Mac source benchmark measures whole fresh processes and has no fleet/gVisor equivalence. V5b's 3.60% paired local improvement is evidence for its narrow source optimization on the frozen scenarios and host. It does not identify either board's rank or explain a purported 15.41x gap.

To compare official versions, read their per-unit trusted records or signed scorer metadata: scorer factory/version, trust profile/rankability, metric domain/clipping, roster/seed identifiers, host fingerprint/resource tier, discarded warmups/measured repeats, output-tree binding and elapsed durations. If those match, compare the documented roster median and paired per-scenario rates; include admissibility/zero floors and batch widths. Until then report the two displayed numbers as different observations with unestablished comparability, not a causal speed ratio.

This was a read-only local source/document review; no browser/network requests, reruns or runtime edits. Primary inspected sources: `Agenthon-2026/03_track3_simulation/qfbench2_track_simulation/{scoring,telemetry,domain}.py`, its README and `ref/notes/track3_simulation.md`.
